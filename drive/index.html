A first-person simulator · Model 3 shaped sedan

CITY DRIVE

  • ✋Steer with your fingers. Put a finger (or two, hand-over-hand) on the steering wheel and turn it. The rim follows your grip 1:1 and self-centres when you let go.
  • 🦶You must use the pedals. Slide up the pedal panels at the bottom corners — travel is analogue, so how far you slide is how hard you press. Lift off to regen-brake.
  • 🅿️Shift out of Park with the P R N D selector on the right, then feed in throttle.
  • 👀Look around by dragging anywhere in the cabin that isn't the wheel. Mirrors are live.
